FAO ECOCROP values are expert-compiled species-level ecological ranges, NOT measured farm data or verified yields. Crop cycle is a broad species-level estimate.
The source_id column is blank when a numeric FAO identifier was not independently confirmed; never invent an ID.
The pH optimal/absolute limits in some mirrored records are identical: preserved as published rather than fabricated.
NASA POWER temperature and seasonal rainfall continue to come from the user's existing nasa_data.py module. Seasonal rainfall MUST NOT be compared with annual FAO rainfall thresholds.
Water categories, soil match, crop rotation and colored ranking remain PROTOTYPE HEURISTICS, not FAO-validated recommendations.
