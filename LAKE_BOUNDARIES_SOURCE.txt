GSHHG intermediate-resolution shoreline/lake polygons, extracted from the Basemap bundled GSHHG coastlines for offline point-in-polygon lake testing. Source: NOAA/NCEI GSHHG (Global Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution Geography Database), https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/shorelines/ . This is a generalized dataset: small lakes and changing shorelines may not be represented. Natural Earth remains the separately bundled low-resolution land baseline.
